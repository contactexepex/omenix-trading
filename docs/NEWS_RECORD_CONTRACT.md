# The news Record contract (rows of the per-story claims)

The per-story claims of the news pipeline, moved here VERBATIM from `docs/RECORD_CONTRACT.md` (one owning document per area, no
document over 500 lines). Read "How to read a row" there first: the columns, the strength words and the plant rule are the same.
`tests/test_record_contract.py` reads both documents as one list of rows and `tools/contract_plants.py` re-runs every plant.

### The stored title (NP9)

The Record was empty when this was built, so nothing was migrated.

## Not guaranteed (news provenance, NP11)

- **A poll staged for a day after that day already has a provenance file** is picked up only by a promotion run whose `--from`/`--to`
  reaches that day (it then becomes a numbered supplement). Until then the cleanup gate keeps the poll staged, so no article is lost,
  and the recall report shows its articles as `no_provenance`. Polls are staged during their own day, so this is rare.
- **Scope is the label the feed had when a pair was written.** A label added later does not relabel old files; the recall report shows
  `with_unknown_feed` so the owner sees how much of a number rests on unlabelled feeds.
- **A closed day with no articles** has no file and is listed again for 9 days; nothing is lost because there was nothing to record.
